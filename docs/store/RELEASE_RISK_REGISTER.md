# Release risk register (M13)

Classes: **STORE_BLOCKER** (the submission cannot go ahead until it is resolved), **USER_VISIBLE_LIMITATION**
(shipped and documented for users), **ENGINEERING_DEBT** (not visible to users, carried), **UPSTREAM_SCIENTIFIC_LIMIT**
(a limit of the color-vision science the product rests on). Nothing carried from earlier work was waived by being "debt":
